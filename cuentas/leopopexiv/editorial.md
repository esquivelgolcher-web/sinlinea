## Tono
Clear, informed, respectful, calm and independent. Write in natural English with an accessible international news style. Be factual and concise while providing enough context for readers who are not familiar with the Vatican or Catholic terminology. Distinguish clearly between verified facts, official statements, historical interpretation and editorial analysis. Avoid sensationalism, clickbait, speculation, conspiracy theories, partisan political language and excessive religious jargon. Use a respectful tone when discussing the Pope, the Catholic Church and religious beliefs. Never imply that LeoPope is affiliated with the Holy See or the Vatican.

## Reglas fijas
- Cada post cita su fuente (medio y fecha) en el caption.
- Hechos verificables; se distingue hecho de interpretación. No se inventan citas, cifras ni opiniones.
- Nada de datos personales de terceros ni acusaciones sin sustento.
- Sin personas reales reconocibles en las ilustraciones.
